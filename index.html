The route

Four stops between your customer data and a sale. Each one is a place campaigns go wrong, and each one is a topic here.

  1. 1

    Data foundations

    Where customer data originates, how it moves between CRM and marketing systems, and whether you can trust it when it gets there.

    Sounds familiar if records don't match between systems, or nobody can say which one is the source of truth.

  2. 2

    Usable audiences

    Segments that reflect today's customers. Queries and filters that stay correct as the data underneath them changes.

    Sounds familiar if audiences are rebuilt by hand or start life as a spreadsheet export.

  3. 3

    Dependable automation

    Scheduling dependencies, error handling, monitoring and recovery, so a failed step gets noticed and fixed before the send.

    Sounds familiar if automations fail quietly and campaigns go out late, or not at all.

  4. 4

    Marketing-to-sales handoffs

    Getting engaged leads into the CRM with the context a salesperson needs to act on them, and proving that it happened.

    Sounds familiar if sales says marketing leads never arrive, or arrive without context.

About Richard

I've spent five years on enterprise Salesforce and Marketing Cloud projects at large consulting companies, in testing, technical and client-facing roles.

The part I like most is the technical layer: the syncs, queries and automations that decide whether a campaign reaches the right person at the right time. Sync & Send is where I write down what I've learned about keeping that layer reliable.
